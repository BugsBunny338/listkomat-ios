Lístkomat 2.6 — what changed in this update:
• Active ticket countdown and expiry: after a purchase, the in-app banner counts
  down to validity ("platí za m:ss" / "valid in m:ss"), then shows the time left
  and "Platí do HH:MM" / "Valid until HH:MM". At expiry the banner turns grey
  ("Jízdenka vypršela" / "Ticket expired"), a local notification announces it,
  and the Live Activity ends showing the expired state. The ticket state is now
  stored by the app itself, so the banner works even with Live Activities turned
  off.
• Location-based city with a manual fallback: the nearest supported city is
  selected from the device location whenever it is available. A city picked by
  hand applies to the current session and is remembered only as the fallback
  for when location is denied or unavailable.
• Readable theme colours: prices and links use a per-theme text colour with at
  least 4.5:1 contrast in every theme, in light and dark mode.
• Unified foreign-SIM notice: the same wording as our Android app, with a
  warning tint and a close (X) button that keeps it dismissed.
• iOS 27 fix: the live map's toolbar buttons now follow theme changes.

Purchase mechanism is UNCHANGED from previous approved versions: a ticket is
bought by sending a carrier-billed premium SMS (price = ticket price), which
requires a Czech SIM. A reviewer on a foreign SIM or without a SIM cannot
complete a purchase — this is expected and not a bug. No in-app purchase applies
(this is a real-world public-transport ticket billed by the carrier).

The live map uses open, CC-BY-licensed public transit data (Prague: Operátor ICT /
Golemio PID; Brno: data.Brno / KORDIS JMK), fetched via a small caching proxy that
holds the data key. The map needs no account, no purchase, and no SIM — a reviewer
can open any city's "Živá mapa" to see it working.

Two things a reviewer will or will not see, both intended:
• The notification permission prompt appears only after a first ticket purchase
  (it is used solely for the expiry notification). Since a purchase cannot be
  completed in review, the prompt will not appear during review.
• With a non-Czech device language or an App Store account outside Czechia, an
  orange "Nákup vyžaduje SIM kartu českého operátora" / "Buying requires a Czech
  operator's SIM" notice sits at the top of the ticket list. That is intended;
  the X dismisses it.

The quickest way to see this release's visible changes: open "Vzhled"
(Appearance, the paintbrush) and switch themes — prices and links stay readable
in each. Open "Vybrat město" (Choose city) — with location allowed, the nearest
city is preselected. "Živá mapa" (Live map) works for Praha and Brno from
anywhere, with no SIM or account.
