Lístkomat 2.5 — what changed in this update:
• Ticket catalog data is now localized: with the device set to English, city
  names (e.g. "Prague"), ticket notes and validity durations appear in English.
  Durations are formatted in the app from the ticket's length in minutes.
• The Brno live map opens faster: vehicle positions are prefetched when the app
  launches or returns to the foreground, and opening the map before the next
  update arrives shows the last known positions in grey (with a "Poslední známé
  polohy · aktualizuji…" / "Last known positions · updating…" note) instead of
  an empty map. Brno's open-data feed sends the whole fleet only about every
  30 seconds, which is what this hides.
• Refreshed app icon — the same backpack artwork, re-rendered larger in the tile.

Purchase mechanism is UNCHANGED from previous approved versions: a ticket is
bought by sending a carrier-billed premium SMS (price = ticket price), which
requires a Czech SIM. A reviewer on a foreign SIM or without a SIM cannot
complete a purchase — this is expected and not a bug. No in-app purchase applies
(this is a real-world public-transport ticket billed by the carrier).

The live map uses open, CC-BY-licensed public transit data (Prague: Operátor ICT /
Golemio PID; Brno: data.Brno / KORDIS JMK), fetched via a small caching proxy that
holds the data key. The map needs no account, no purchase, and no SIM — a reviewer
can open any city's "Živá mapa" to see it working.

The quickest way to see this release's visible changes: set the device language
to English — the city reads "Prague" and the ticket notes and durations are in
English. For the live map: with Brno selected, open "Živá mapa" (Live map) and
wait for the vehicles, then send the app to the background for a few seconds,
return, and reopen the map — the last known positions appear immediately in
grey, then turn to colour when the next update lands (up to ~30 seconds).
