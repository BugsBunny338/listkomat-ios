Lístkomat 2.7 — what changed in this update (a polish release):
• Cities without a live map (e.g. Ostrava, Hradec Králové) now say so with one
  line under the ticket list: "Pro toto město zatím živou mapu nemáme." /
  "No live map for this city yet."
• The active ticket's name ("Praha · 30 minut" / "Prague · 30 minutes") is now
  worded in the current app language whenever it is shown, also after a
  language switch.
• Readability and accessibility: button labels on theme-coloured fills switch to
  black or white, whichever reads; the banner's "waiting for confirmation SMS"
  caption, its "Ukončit"/"End" button and the Live Activity caption are shaded
  to at least 4.5:1 contrast; VoiceOver no longer reads the decorative tram
  icon.
• The mascot easter egg (tap the emoji at the top left on any theme with a
  mascot) rains more smoothly, and is off when Reduce Motion is on.

Purchase mechanism is UNCHANGED from previous approved versions: a ticket is
bought by sending a carrier-billed premium SMS (price = ticket price), which
requires a Czech SIM. A reviewer on a foreign SIM or without a SIM cannot
complete a purchase — this is expected and not a bug. No in-app purchase applies
(this is a real-world public-transport ticket billed by the carrier).

The live map uses open, CC-BY-licensed public transit data (Prague: Operátor ICT /
Golemio PID; Brno: data.Brno / KORDIS JMK), fetched via a small caching proxy that
holds the data key. The map needs no account, no purchase, and no SIM — a reviewer
can open any city's "Živá mapa" to see it working.

Two things a reviewer will or will not see, both intended:
• The notification permission prompt appears only after a first ticket purchase
  (it is used solely for the expiry notification). Since a purchase cannot be
  completed in review, the prompt will not appear during review.
• With a non-Czech device language or an App Store account outside Czechia, an
  orange "Nákup vyžaduje SIM kartu českého operátora" / "Buying requires a Czech
  operator's SIM" notice sits at the top of the ticket list. That is intended;
  the X dismisses it.

The quickest way to see this release's visible changes: choose a city without a
live map in "Vybrat město" (Choose city), e.g. Ostrava — the new line sits under
the ticket list. Open "Vzhled" (Appearance, the paintbrush), pick a theme with a
mascot (e.g. "Bomba") and tap the mascot at the top left. "Živá mapa" (Live map)
works for Praha and Brno from anywhere, with no SIM or account.
