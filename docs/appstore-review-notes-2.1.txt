Lístkomat 2.1 — what changed in this update:
• Live transit map now covers Prague (in addition to Brno) — real-time trams,
  metro, buses, trains and trolleybuses, with the vehicle's destination shown on tap.
• The app version number now appears in the Appearance settings.

Purchase mechanism is UNCHANGED from previous approved versions: a ticket is
bought by sending a carrier-billed premium SMS (price = ticket price), which
requires a Czech SIM. A reviewer on a foreign SIM or without a SIM cannot
complete a purchase — this is expected and not a bug. No in-app purchase applies
(this is a real-world public-transport ticket billed by the carrier).

The live map uses open, CC-BY-licensed public transit data (Prague: Operátor ICT /
Golemio PID; Brno: data.Brno / KORDIS JMK), fetched via a small caching proxy that
holds the data key. The map needs no account, no purchase, and no SIM — a reviewer
can open any city's "Živá mapa" to see it working.
